Privacy Policy

★ How WizardFrac takes care of your information ★

About WizardFrac

WizardFrac is a fraction-learning game made as a school capstone project. This page explains what information the game keeps and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details you enter when you sign up: your nickname, your password (stored scrambled, never as plain text) and, if you add one, a profile picture.
  • Your game progress: levels, scores, answers, hints used and time spent, so the dashboard can show how you are learning.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To show your progress on your dashboard.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as your progress report (PDF) or a password reset. The email address you type in is only used to send that email.

Email

WizardFrac sends emails through Google's Gmail API. It only has permission to send emails. It cannot read, change or delete anyone's inbox. Emails are only sent when you ask for them.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. It is stored only so the game can work, using the services that host it (Vercel, Render and Supabase).

Deleting your data

You can ask the WizardFrac team to delete your account and progress at any time, and we will remove it.
